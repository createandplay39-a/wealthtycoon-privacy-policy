📋 Introduction

Welcome to WealthTycoon. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By playing WealthTycoon, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🔍 Information We Collect

Short answer: WealthTycoon itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, location, or any device content. Your game progress stays on your device. However, WealthTycoon shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and that advertising service does collect certain device and advertising data on our behalf, as described below.

Directly, WealthTycoon does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Precise location data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Photos, contacts, or any other device content

However, because WealthTycoon uses Google AdMob to deliver optional rewarded video ads, AdMob automatically collects certain information whenever an ad is loaded or shown, including:

  • Advertising ID (and, on newer devices, similar limited-tracking identifiers)
  • General device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • IP address (used to derive an approximate, non-precise location such as country/region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not stored on our own servers, and is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud/abuse prevention, measurement, and — where you have given consent — ad personalization. See the Advertising & Third-Party Services section below for details and the Your Ad Choices section for how to control this.

💾 Local Storage & Save Data

Your game progress (cash, investments, achievements, prestige level, etc.) is saved exclusively on your own device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data through your device's settings.

🔗 Advertising & Third-Party Services

WealthTycoon uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (for example, to unlock certain upgrades faster). We do not use any analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or social-platform integrations — AdMob is currently the only third-party service integrated into the app.

When an ad is requested, loaded, or shown, Google AdMob may collect and process the data listed in the Information We Collect section, and may share it with Google and its advertising partners for the purposes described there. Google's own collection and use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and, for how Google's advertising products work, Google's Advertising Technologies page.

Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), we obtain consent for personalized advertising through a certified Consent Management Platform before any personalized ad data is collected or used. If you do not consent, you will still be shown ads, but they will not be personalized.

If future updates add other third-party services (such as analytics or leaderboards), this policy will be updated before those features are released.

🎛️ Your Ad Choices

You can control ad personalization and the use of your advertising identifier at any time:

  • On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID.
  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage how Google personalizes ads across the services you use.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all further ad-related data collection by AdMob within WealthTycoon.

Opting out of ad personalization does not stop ads from being shown — it only means the ads you see will no longer be based on your interests.

👶 Children's Privacy

WealthTycoon is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age threshold under local law, such as under 16 in parts of the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly design the game for, or knowingly collect personal data from, children in that age group.

Because the app serves ads through Google AdMob, some device and advertising data described in Information We Collect is processed even for general audiences. If we become aware that a child under the applicable age has provided personal data to us directly, we will take steps to delete it. If our app's listed target audience on the app store includes children, ads are configured to serve only non-personalized, contextual advertising to comply with Google Play Families Policies and applicable laws such as COPPA.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's use of the game or about data collected via advertising, please contact us using the information below.

⚖️ Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over the data described in this policy — for example, the right to know what is collected, to object to processing, or to request deletion. Because WealthTycoon does not maintain user accounts or its own servers, most of this data is held by Google as described above; you can exercise your choices directly through the Your Ad Choices controls, or contact us and we will help direct your request or answer questions about our use of AdMob.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the game after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
